# government

## Definition
Actions by government, policy or regulatory bodies, or legal rulings, with implications beyond a single firm.

## Rules
- R1: Answer yes for legislation, regulation, tariffs, export controls, antitrust actions or court rulings that apply beyond a single firm.
- R2: Answer no for a lawsuit or regulatory matter that concerns only one company and sets no wider precedent.
- R3: Proposed or pending actions count when the email names the body involved and the action under consideration.
- R4: Central bank rate decisions belong under macro, not here.
