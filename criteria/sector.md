# sector

## Definition
Developments that affect the sector or industry and are not limited to one target company.

## Rules
- R1: Answer yes when the email describes a development affecting several firms in an industry, such as supply chain shifts, industry pricing or technology adoption.
- R2: Answer no when the development concerns only one company's own results, products or management.
- R3: Research on a peer outside the five counts only if the email draws a read-through to the wider industry.
