# affected_tickers

## Definition
A target company is listed when it is meaningfully affected by the email's content. A company mentioned only in passing is not affected. Use GOOGL for Alphabet and Google.

## Rules
- R1: List a company when the email's content bears on its revenue, costs, competition, regulation or valuation, whether the effect is favorable or unfavorable.
- R2: Do not list a company that is only named in a list, a signature, an example or an email about something else.
- R3: List a company affected through a stated read-through, such as a supplier, customer or competitor development, even if it is not the main subject.
- R4: Treat products, subsidiaries and divisions as their parent, such as YouTube as GOOGL and AWS as AMZN.
