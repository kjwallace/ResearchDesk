# irrelevant

## Definition
No meaningful investment connection to the target companies.

## Rules
- R1: Choose this for research on companies outside the five, however strong, unless the email states a read-through to a target company.
- R2: Credible macroeconomic or legal commentary belongs here when the email gives no plausible effect on any of the five.
- R3: An email that names a target company only in passing while discussing something else belongs here when nothing it says affects that company.
- R4: Personal, administrative and purely promotional emails with no target company content belong here.
