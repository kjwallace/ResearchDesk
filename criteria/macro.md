# macro

## Definition
Broader macroeconomic events, data or trends, such as inflation, rates, currency or global demand.

## Rules
- R1: Answer yes when the email discusses economy-wide data or policy, such as inflation, interest rates, employment, currency moves or global demand.
- R2: Answer no when demand or pricing trends are specific to one industry; those belong under sector.
- R3: A passing mention of the economy in an email about something else does not count.
