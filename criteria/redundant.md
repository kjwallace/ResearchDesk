# redundant

## Definition
Information already known that adds no meaningful incremental insight. This includes later emails that repeat an earlier email in the same day.

## Rules
- R1: Choose this when the email restates widely reported results, guidance or announcements without adding a new figure, source or implication.
- R2: Thorough or well-written analysis still belongs here if every fact and conclusion it offers is already consensus.
- R3: Recaps, digests and summaries of public news belong here unless they contain at least one item that is not already widely known.
