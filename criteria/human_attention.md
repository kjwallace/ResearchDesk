# human_attention

## Definition
The email contains a request, meeting or opportunity that needs a person on the desk to act, from a credible and valuable source: for example a small-group meeting with company management, a call offered by a differentiated analyst or industry expert, or a time-sensitive question from a trusted contact. All sales invitations, generic networking requests and mass conference promotions do not qualify.

## Rules
- R1: Answer yes only when the email asks the desk to do something specific, such as accept a meeting, join a call or reply to a question.
- R2: Answer no for well-known conferences, broad investor days and routine analyst meetings that offer no small-group or differentiated access.
- R3: Answer no when the email only shares information or research, however useful, and asks for no action.
- R4: A deadline or urgent subject line alone does not make a request qualify; judge the access offered and the source's role.
