# thesis_relevant

## Definition
New, specific information that would support, challenge or change a reasonable investment thesis on a target company: its earnings expectations, competitive outlook, valuation or a trading decision.

## Rules
- R1: Choose this when the email reports a confirmed, specific fact such as a figure, guidance change, order, ruling or first-hand observation; early or unconfirmed signals belong in monitor.
- R2: The fact must be new rather than consensus; careful analysis that only restates widely known figures or guidance belongs in redundant.
- R3: Judge only the information itself, not sender prestige, writing quality, length or formatting; a short informal note with one material first-hand fact qualifies.
- R4: When one material item sits inside promotional or unrelated material, judge the email by that item and ignore the rest.
