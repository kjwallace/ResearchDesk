# low_value

## Definition
Some connection to a target company, but insufficient materiality, differentiation or evidentiary support to merit attention.

## Rules
- R1: Choose this when a target company is genuinely connected to the content but the information is minor, generic or offered without supporting evidence.
- R2: Detailed, data-heavy reports that draw no conclusion about a target company belong here, however much data they contain.
- R3: Unsourced rumors, speculative headlines framed as questions, personal hunches and teasers belong here even when they hint at a target company.
- R4: Pitches for data products, free samples, trials, subscriptions or paid consulting belong here unless they contain a specific, substantive finding about a target company.
- R5: Sender prestige, polished writing, length, formatting or a dramatic subject line never raise an email above this label on their own.
- R6: Mainly promotional emails that mention a target company without a substantive item belong here; with no real connection to the five, choose irrelevant.
